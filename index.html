About

Design it, build it, then go break it.

I'm in the Bachelor's–Accelerated Master's program at CU Boulder, finishing a BS in mechanical engineering with a computer science minor in 2027 and an MS in 2028. The CS minor is deliberate: the projects I care about tend to sit where mechanical design meets control, sensing, and software.

That's played out in practice — designing fiber optic hardware in SolidWorks at a startup, leading electrical and firmware work on a GPS beacon, and spending two summers at Pure Fishing building AI systems that shipped to real customers. In between I weld frames, run FEA, and race a drill-powered vehicle around a track.

Right now I'm looking for a Summer 2027 mechanical engineering internship where I can be in CAD and on the shop floor in the same week.
