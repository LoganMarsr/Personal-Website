02 / MCEN 1025 — Computer Aided Design

Stovetop Popcorn Popper

SolidWorks·Full assembly + exploded view·Spring 2024

A complete SolidWorks assembly of a crank-driven stovetop popcorn popper, modeled part by part. The interesting work is in the lid: a sheet-metal stamping carrying a gear pair, a stirring arm, and a folding crank that all have to clear each other through a full rotation.

  • Modeled every component individually — pot, sheet-metal lid, gear pair, stirring linkage, and turned wooden handles.
  • Built a mating assembly with the correct degrees of freedom so the crank and stirrer rotate together.
  • Produced an exploded view documenting assembly order and the full bill of materials.
  • Applied realistic appearances and lighting for presentation renders.
  • SolidWorks
  • Assembly Mates
